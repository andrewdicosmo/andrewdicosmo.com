CASE-053 // The ProductTwo Thesis

Watch the platform run both sides of a business. One cycle, a customer arrives from a web form, the marketplace, or a referral, and the system carries them from lead to CRM to quote to customer, with nurture emails that grow the relationship and an invoice at the end. The next cycle, it's a candidate: people find jobs on ProductTwo, companies hire through it as W2 or C2C, and the entire process from application to onboarding is tracked in one place. Underneath both, AI digital employees work the pipelines, sourcing and matching, drafting the emails, building the plans, all on the same system, and you only pay for what you use. Built in: CRM, ERP, marketplace, ATS, invoicing, marketing automation, CDP, DAM, and a two way Shopify integration. In build at example.com.
